Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · VolteraX Hardware Systems
VOLTERAX ZERO-SALE DATA GUARANTEE: VolteraX collects customer contact and dispatch credentials strictly to assemble, program, test, and ship your engineering systems. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

To fulfill electronics prototype commissions and kits, VolteraX collects:

  • Client Identity: Full name, institutional or company affiliation, and academic details if ordering for research.
  • Direct Contact: 10-digit Indian mobile number (for OTP authentication, order dispatch updates, and WhatsApp technical discussions) and email address.
  • Logistics Data: Physical shipping address, district, state, and 6-digit Indian postal pincode for express courier transit.
  • Project Technical Specifications: Component requirements, microcontroller choices, schematic parameters, and custom firmware briefs submitted via our project order system.

2. How We Utilize Your Information

  • Engineering and fabricating your requested custom electronics, IoT systems, or robotics prototypes.
  • Dispatching your hardware consignment through tracked logistics partners (BlueDart, Delhivery, DTDC, India Post) and sharing tracking Air Waybill (AWB) numbers via SMS/WhatsApp.
  • Providing remote hardware debugging, pinout explanations, and technical setup guidance post-delivery.
  • Communicating critical build milestone updates or component availability notifications.

3. Data Protection & Security Controls

All passwords stored in our system are salted and cryptographically hashed using Scrypt algorithms (NIST-compliant). Account tokens are transmitted strictly over TLS/SSL (HTTPS) connections with HttpOnly, SameSite security flags. We implement strict server-side authorization boundaries on all customer records.

4. Payment Information Security

VolteraX processes all digital transactions via PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment gateways (Razorpay / Cashfree / UPI). VolteraX never stores or receives your credit card, debit card, CVV, UPI PIN, or bank passwords on our servers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have full authority to request deletion or modification of your account credentials at any time. Simply email VolteraX.360@gmail.com with your registered mobile number, and your records will be purged within 48 hours.
