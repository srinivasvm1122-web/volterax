VOLTERAX / REFUND POLICY

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear, transparent policies for custom prototypes and project kits
MANDATORY TRANSIT DAMAGE REQUIREMENT: To claim a free replacement or repair for transit damage, customers must record a continuous, uncut 360-degree unboxing video from the unopened outer courier packaging through opening and initial inspection.

1. Advance Payment & Custom Commission Policy

For custom engineering prototypes and made-to-order hardware assemblies, VolteraX requires a 50% non-refundable advance deposit before hardware assembly commences. This deposit is allocated immediately to procure specialized microcontrollers, sensors, modules, and custom chassis components tailored to your project.

2. Cancellation Timeline

  • Ready-Made Project Kits: Cancellation requests are accepted within 6 hours of order placement, provided the consignment has not been handed over to the courier partner. A full refund will be processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Custom Prototype Builds: Once hardware assembly, circuit wiring, PCB layout etching, or soldering has commenced, no cancellations or refunds can be entertained under any circumstances.

3. Free Transit Damage Replacement Guarantee

Every VolteraX build is packaged with multi-layer anti-static cushioning, bubble wrap, and rigid outer enclosures. In the rare event that courier transit causes physical or structural damage:

The uncut unboxing video must be submitted to VolteraX.360@gmail.com or our official WhatsApp support within 24 hours of delivery. Upon verification, VolteraX will dispatch replacement modules or arrange free return pickup and re-assembly at our cost.

4. Technical Assistance & Defect Resolution

If a module fails to operate according to provided technical documentation, our core engineering team provides dedicated remote technical assistance via WhatsApp or Google Meet within 48 hours to troubleshoot code, wiring, and power supply issues.
